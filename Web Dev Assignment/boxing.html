Definition of Boxing

Boxing is a combat sport in which two participants, usually wearing protective gloves and headgear, throw punches at each other for a predetermined number of rounds in a boxing ring. The objective is to score points by landing punches on the opponent or to win by knockout.

Five Basic Rules of Boxing

  1. Scoring System:
    Points are awarded for clean punches landed on the opponent's head or upper body. Judges score each round, and the boxer with the most points wins if there is no knockout.
  2. Knockout (KO):
    A boxer wins if their opponent is unable to get up after being knocked down and counted out by the referee (usually a count of 10).
  3. Technical Knockout (TKO):
    A referee or ringside physician can stop the fight if a boxer is unable to defend themselves properly or is deemed unfit to continue.
  4. Weight Classes:
    Boxers compete in different weight classes to ensure fair competition, such as heavyweight, middleweight, and lightweight.
  5. Fouls:
    Illegal moves such as hitting below the belt, headbutting, and hitting after the bell are prohibited and can result in point deductions or disqualification.

Five Prestigious Boxing Awards Globally

  1. Olympic Medals:
    Winning a gold, silver, or bronze medal at the Olympic Games is one of the highest honors in amateur boxing.

  2. Titles awarded by major boxing organizations such as the WBA (World Boxing Association), WBC (World Boxing Council), IBF (International Boxing Federation), and WBO (World Boxing Organization).
  3. Ring Magazine Fighter of the Year:
    An annual award given to the best professional boxer of the year.
  4. ESPY Award for Best Boxer:
    An annual award presented by ESPN to recognize the best boxer in a given year.
  5. Hall of Fame Inductions:
    Being inducted into the International Boxing Hall of Fame is a lifetime achievement for boxers who have made significant contributions to the sport.

Five of India's Achievements in Boxing


  1. Mary Kom: Six-time world champion and bronze medalist at the 2012 London Olympics.

  2. Vijender Singh: Bronze medalist at the 2008 Beijing Olympics and multiple-time Asian Games medalist.

  3. Shiva Thapa: Youngest Indian boxer to qualify for the Olympics and multiple-time Asian Championships medalist.

  4. Amit Panghal: Silver medalist at the 2019 World Championships and gold medalist at the 2018 Asian Games.
  5. Gaurav Bidhuri:
    Bronze medalist at the 2017 World Championships.
