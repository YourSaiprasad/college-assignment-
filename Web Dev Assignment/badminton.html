Definition of Badminton

Badminton is a racquet sport in which players hit a shuttlecock back and forth across a net. The objective is to score points by landing the shuttlecock in the opponent's half of the court.

Five Rules of Badminton

Service:
The serve must be delivered in an upward direction with an underhand stroke, and it must land in the opposite service box.

Scoring System:
Each match is best of three games, each game up to 21 points. Players must win by two points if the score reaches 20-20.

Faults:
Common faults include hitting the shuttlecock out of bounds, into the net, or allowing it to hit the floor on the player's side.

Let:
A let is called when the play is interrupted or the shuttlecock gets stuck in the net after crossing it.

Court Boundaries:
During play, the shuttlecock must land within the court boundaries to be considered in. For singles, the side tramlines are out; for doubles, they are in.

Five Prestigious Awards in Badminton Globally

All England Open Badminton Championships:

One of the oldest and most prestigious tournaments in the world, held annually in England.

World Championships:
Organized by the Badminton World Federation (BWF), this is the highest level of competition in badminton.

Thomas Cup:
The world team championship for men, held every two years.

Uber Cup:
The world team championship for women, held every two years.

Olympic Medals:
Winning an Olympic medal is considered one of the highest honors in badminton.

Five of India's Achievements in Badminton

  1. P. V. Sindhu:
    Won a silver medal at the Rio 2016 Olympics and a bronze medal at the Tokyo 2020 Olympics.
  2. Saina Nehwal:
    Secured a bronze medal at the London 2012 Olympics.
  3. Pullela Gopichand:
    Won the All England Open Badminton Championships in 2001.
  4. Kidambi Srikanth:
    Became the first Indian male to achieve the world number 1 ranking in 2018.
  5. Satwiksairaj Rankireddy and Chirag Shetty:
    The first Indian men's doubles pair to win a Super Series title, with notable victories in tournaments like the Thailand Open.
