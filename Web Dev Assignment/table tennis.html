Definition of Table Tennis

Table tennis, also known as ping-pong, is an indoor game based on tennis, played with small bats and a ball bounced on a table divided by a net.

Five Basic Rules of Table Tennis

  1. Serve:
    The ball must be thrown at least 15 cm in the air and hit so it bounces on the server's side first, then the opponent's side.
  2. Return:
    The opponent must return the ball so it first bounces on their side and then over the net.
  3. Let:
    If the ball hits the net during a rally but still lands on the opponent's side, it's a "let" and the serve is replayed.
  4. Fault:
    If the ball doesn't bounce on the server's side first, or if the server steps on the table, it's a fault.
  5. Scoring:
    A game is played to 11 points, and a match is usually the best of five games.

Five Prestigious Table Tennis Awards Globally

  1. World Table Tennis Championships: Held biennially, it's the most prestigious tournament in table tennis.
  2. Olympic Gold Medal:
    Table tennis has been an Olympic sport since 1988.
  3. ITTF World Tour Grand Finals:
    The top players compete in this annual event.
  4. Asian Games Gold Medal:
    A major regional competition for table tennis.
  5. ITTF Hall of Fame:
    Honors the greatest players in the sport's history.

Five Achievements of India in Table Tennis

  1. Nikhil Kumar's Bronze Medal at the 2020 Tokyo Olympics:
    India's first Olympic medal in table tennis.
  2. Sharath Kamal's Multiple National Championships:
    Sharath Kamal has won numerous national titles and is a prominent figure in Indian table tennis.
  3. Indian Men's Team's Bronze at the 2021 World Table Tennis Championships:
    A historic achievement for Indian table tennis.
  4. Indian Women's Team's Success in Asian Championships:
    Consistent performances and medals in the Asian Table Tennis Championships.
  5. Rising Rankings:
    Indian players have been steadily climbing the world rankings, showcasing the growth of the sport in the country.
