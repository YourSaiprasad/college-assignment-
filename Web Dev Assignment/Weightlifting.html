Definition of Weightlifting

Weightlifting is a sport in which athletes attempt to lift heavy weights mounted on barbells in a single lift or series of lifts. It includes two main competitive lifts: the snatch and the clean and jerk. The objective is to lift the heaviest weights possible within the athlete's weight class.

Five Basic Rules of Weightlifting

  1. Lift Types:
    There are two main types of lifts in Olympic weightlifting - the snatch, and the clean and jerk. Each athlete gets three attempts at each lift.
  2. Weight Classes:
    Athletes compete in different weight classes to ensure fair competition. Each class has its own records and standards.
  3. Judging:
    Three judges observe each lift. A majority decision (2 out of 3) determines if the lift is successful.
  4. Timing:
    Athletes have one minute to start their lift after their name is called. If a lift is failed, the athlete must attempt the next weight within a specified time.
  5. Disqualification:
    Any body part other than the feet touching the platform, failing to lock out the arms in the snatch or clean and jerk, or dropping the bar before the referee's signal results in a failed lift.

Five Prestigious Weightlifting Awards Globally

  1. Olympic Medals:
    Gold, silver, and bronze medals awarded at the Olympic Games are among the highest honors in weightlifting.
  2. World Weightlifting Championships Medals:
    Winning medals at the World Championships is a significant achievement.
  3. Pan American Games Medals:
    Prestigious medals awarded at this major multi-sport event for athletes from the Americas.
  4. Commonwealth Games Medals:
    Medals awarded at this multi-sport event, particularly significant for athletes from the Commonwealth nations.
  5. European Weightlifting Championships Medals:
    Medals awarded at this premier event for European athletes.

Five of India's Achievements in Weightlifting

  1. Karnam Malleswari:
    Won a bronze medal at the 2000 Sydney Olympics, becoming the first Indian woman to win an Olympic medal in weightlifting.
  2. Saikhom Mirabai Chanu:
    Won a silver medal at the 2020 Tokyo Olympics in the women's 49kg category.
  3. Sathish Sivalingam:
    Won gold medals at the 2014 and 2018 Commonwealth Games in the men's 77kg and 77kg categories respectively.
  4. Ragala Venkat Rahul:
    Won gold at the 2018 Commonwealth Games in the men's 85kg category.
  5. Sanjita Chanu:
    Achieved gold medals at the 2014 and 2018 Commonwealth Games in the women's 48kg and 53kg categories respectively.
