Definition of Tennis

Tennis is a racket sport that can be played individually against a single opponent (singles) or between two teams of two players each (doubles). The game involves hitting a ball over a net and into the opponent's court with the objective of winning points by making the ball bounce twice in the opponent's court or forcing an error.

Five Basic Rules in Tennis

  1. Scoring:
    Matches are scored in sets and games. A player needs to win six games to win a set and typically needs to win by two sets to win a match.
  2. Service Rules:
    Each game begins with a serve from behind the baseline. The server must alternate sides after each point and must serve diagonally into the opponent's service box.
  3. In/Out:
    The ball must land within the court boundaries. If the ball lands outside the lines, it is considered out, and the point goes to the opponent.
  4. No Touch:
    Players cannot touch the net with their body or racket during play. Doing so results in losing the point.
  5. Let Calls:
    If a served ball hits the net but lands in the correct service box, it is called a "let," and the serve is retaken.

Five Major Global Competitions in Tennis

  1. The Grand Slam Tournaments:
    The four most prestigious events are the Australian Open, French Open, Wimbledon, and US Open.
  2. ATP and WTA Tours:
    Annual circuits of tournaments for professional male (ATP) and female (WTA) players.
  3. Davis Cup:
    An international team event for men, organized by the International Tennis Federation (ITF).
  4. Fed Cup:
    The premier international team competition in women's tennis, also organized by the ITF.
  5. Olympic Games:
    Tennis is a featured sport at the Summer Olympics, with medals awarded in various categories.

Five Notable Indian Achievements in Tennis

  1. Leander Paes:
    Won an Olympic bronze medal in singles at the 1996 Atlanta Olympics and has numerous Grand Slam titles in doubles and mixed doubles.
  2. Mahesh Bhupathi:
    The first Indian to win a Grand Slam title, he has multiple Grand Slam victories in doubles and mixed doubles.
  3. Sania Mirza:
    The most successful Indian female tennis player, she has won multiple Grand Slam titles in doubles and mixed doubles.
  4. Rohan Bopanna:
    Achieved success in doubles with multiple ATP titles and a Grand Slam mixed doubles title at the French Open.
  5. Indian Davis Cup Team:
    Reached the Davis Cup finals three times (1966, 1974, and 1987), showcasing strong team performances over the years.
