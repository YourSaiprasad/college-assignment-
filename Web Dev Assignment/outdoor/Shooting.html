Definition of Shooting

Shooting is a sport that involves firing a firearm or airgun at a target to score points based on accuracy and precision. The sport includes various disciplines such as rifle shooting, pistol shooting, and shotgun shooting, with events held at different distances and positions.

Five Basic Rules in Shooting

  1. Safety First:
    Always follow safety protocols, including handling firearms carefully, wearing protective gear, and following range commands.
  2. Correct Positioning:
    In rifle and pistol events, shooters must adopt the correct shooting positions (prone, standing, or kneeling) as specified by the competition rules.
  3. Time Limits:
    Shooters must complete their shots within a specified time limit for each event or series of shots.
  4. Scoring:
    Points are awarded based on the accuracy of the shots, with the highest scores given for shots closest to the center of the target.
  5. Equipment Regulations:
    Firearms and ammunition used in competitions must comply with specific regulations regarding caliber, weight, and modifications.

Five Major Global Competitions in Shooting

  1. Olympic Games:
    Shooting is a prominent sport in the Olympics, with events for rifle, pistol, and shotgun disciplines.
  2. ISSF World Cup:
    The International Shooting Sport Federation (ISSF) organizes the World Cup series, featuring top shooters from around the world.
  3. ISSF World Championships:
    Held every four years, this event showcases the best shooters in various disciplines competing for world titles.
  4. Commonwealth Games:
    Shooting is a key event in the Commonwealth Games, featuring participants from member countries.
  5. Asian Games:
    Shooting events are an integral part of the Asian Games, with top shooters from across the continent competing.

Five Notable Indian Achievements in Shooting

  1. Abhinav Bindra:
    Won India's first individual Olympic gold medal in the 10m air rifle event at the 2008 Beijing Olympics.
  2. Rajyavardhan Singh Rathore:
    Won the silver medal in the men's double trap event at the 2004 Athens Olympics.
  3. Gagan Narang:
    Won the bronze medal in the 10m air rifle event at the 2012 London Olympics.
  4. Manu Bhaker:
    Young shooting prodigy who has won multiple gold medals in ISSF World Cups and the Youth Olympics.
  5. Rahi Sarnobat:
    Won the gold medal in the 25m pistol event at the 2018 Asian Games, becoming the first Indian woman to do so.
