Definition of Hockey

Hockey is a sport where two teams play against each other by trying to maneuver a ball or a puck into the opponent's goal using a hockey stick. There are various forms of hockey, including field hockey, ice hockey, and roller hockey.

Five Basic Rules in Field Hockey

  1. Stick Rules:
    Players can only use the flat side of their stick to hit the ball.
  2. Scoring:
    A goal is scored when the ball is hit from inside the striking circle and passes entirely over the goal line between the goalposts and under the crossbar.
  3. Fouls:
    Any play deemed dangerous, such as raising the stick above the shoulder, using the stick to trip an opponent, or striking the ball in a way that could injure another player, is a foul.
  4. Penalty Corners:
    Awarded for defensive fouls within the striking circle. A set play is taken from the 10-yard mark on the backline.
  5. Player Substitution:
    Teams can substitute players at any time, including during play, as long as the player being substituted comes off the field before the new player enters.

Five Major Global Competitions in Field Hockey

  1. Olympic Games:
    Held every four years, hockey is one of the key events.
  2. FIH Hockey World Cup:
    The premier field hockey championship, held every four years for men and women.
  3. FIH Hockey Pro League:
    An annual global league competition for men's and women's national teams.
  4. Champions Trophy:
    A prestigious tournament featuring the top-ranked national teams.
  5. Commonwealth Games:
    A multi-sport event held every four years, including field hockey as one of the sports.

Five Notable Indian Achievements in Hockey

  1. Dhyan Chand:
    Known as "The Wizard," he led India to three Olympic gold medals in 1928, 1932, and 1936.
  2. 1975 World Cup:
    India won its only Men's Hockey World Cup in 1975.
  3. Recent FIH Pro League Success:
    The Indian men's hockey team finished third in the 2020–21 season.
  4. Women's Hockey Team at Tokyo 2020:
    The Indian women's team reached the semi-finals for the first time, finishing fourth.
  5. 2018 Asian Games:
    Indian men’s team won the gold medal, and the women’s team won the silver medal.
