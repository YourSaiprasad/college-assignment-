Definition of Golf

Golf is a club-and-ball sport in which players use various clubs to hit a ball into a series of holes on a course in as few strokes as possible. The modern game originated in 15th century Scotland1.

Five Basic Rules of Golf

  1. Play the Ball as It Lies:
    You must play the ball from where it lands, unless it's out of bounds or in a penalty area.
  2. Teeing Ground:
    Tee the ball behind the markers on the tee box.
  3. Stroke Play:
    Count all your strokes and aim to complete the hole with the fewest strokes
    4. .
  4. Out of Bounds:
    If your ball goes out of bounds, you must replay it from the last spot with a penalty stroke.
  5. Order of Play:
    The player furthest from the hole plays first.
