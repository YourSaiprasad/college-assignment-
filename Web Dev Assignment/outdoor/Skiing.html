Definition of Skiing

Skiing is a sport or recreational activity that involves gliding over snow using skis, which are long, flat runners attached to the boots. Skiing can be done on various terrains such as alpine slopes, cross-country trails, or through mountainous regions.

Five Basic Rules in Skiing

  1. Obey All Signs and Markings:
    Follow posted signs and markings to ensure safety on the slopes.
  2. Yield to Others:
    The skier ahead of you has the right of way. You must avoid them.
  3. Control Your Speed:
    Always ski in control and at a speed that is safe for your skill level and the trail conditions.

  4. Stop Safely:Only stop on the slope where you are visible to others and not blocking the trail.
  5. Use Proper Equipment:
    Ensure your skis, bindings, and other equipment are in good condition and suitable for your skiing activity.

Five Major Global Competitions in Skiing

  1. Winter Olympic Games:
    Held every four years, featuring various skiing events like alpine skiing, cross-country skiing, and freestyle skiing.
  2. FIS Alpine World Ski Championships:
    Biennial event organized by the International Ski Federation (FIS), featuring the best alpine skiers.
  3. FIS Cross-Country World Cup:
    An annual series of cross-country skiing competitions held at different locations worldwide.
  4. Freeride World Tour:
    A global series of freeride skiing and snowboarding events held on challenging backcountry terrain.
  5. X Games:
    An annual extreme sports event featuring freestyle skiing and other snow sports.

Five Notable Indian Achievements in Skiing

  1. Aanchal Thakur:
    Won India's first international skiing medal at the Alpine Ejder 3200 Cup in Turkey (2018).
  2. Himanshu Thakur:
    Represented India at the 2014 Winter Olympics in Sochi, competing in alpine skiing events.
  3. Arif Khan:
    Qualified for the 2022 Winter Olympics in Beijing, becoming the first Indian to compete in both alpine skiing and slalom events.
  4. Nadeem Iqbal:
    Competed in cross-country skiing at the 2014 Winter Olympics, representing India.
  5. Gul Mustafa Dev:
    Known as a pioneer of winter sports in India, promoting skiing and establishing training facilities in Jammu and Kashmir.
