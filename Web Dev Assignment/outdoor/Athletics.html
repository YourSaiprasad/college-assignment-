Definition of Athletics

Athletics is a collection of sporting events that involve competitive running, jumping, throwing, and walking. It is considered one of the oldest forms of organized sport and includes events like sprints, marathons, relays, long jump, high jump, shot put, and more.

Five Rules in Athletics

  1. False Start Rule:
    In sprint races, any athlete who false starts (begins running before the start signal) is disqualified after one warning.
  2. Lane Infringement:
    In events like sprints and relays, athletes must stay within their designated lanes. Stepping on or over the lane lines results in disqualification.
  3. Throwing Technique:
    For events like shot put, javelin, and discus, athletes must adhere to specific techniques and release the implement from within designated areas.
  4. Jumping Standards:
    In long jump and triple jump, athletes must take off from behind the foul line. Any jump with a foot over the line is invalid.
  5. Race Walking:
    Competitors must maintain contact with the ground at all times and the supporting leg must straighten from the point of contact until in a vertical position.

    6. Five Global Competitions in Athletics

  1. Olympic Games:
    Held every four years, the Olympics are the pinnacle of global athletic competition.
  2. World Athletics Championships:
    This biennial event organized by World Athletics features the best athletes from around the world.
  3. Diamond League:
    An annual series of elite track and field meetings where top athletes compete in different events.
  4. Commonwealth Games:
    Held every four years, this multi-sport event includes countries from the Commonwealth of Nations.
  5. European Championships:
    A biennial event featuring athletes from European countries competing in various track and field disciplines.

    6. Five Indian Achievements in Athletics

  1. Neeraj Chopra:
    2. Won the gold medal in javelin throw at the Tokyo 2020 Olympics, becoming India's first Olympic gold medalist in athletics.
  2. Milkha Singh:
    3. Known as "The Flying Sikh," he won gold in the 400 meters at the 1958 Commonwealth Games.
  3. PT Usha:
    4. Dominated Asian athletics in the 1980s, winning multiple gold medals at Asian Games and setting several national records.
  4. Anju Bobby George:
    5. Won the bronze medal in long jump at the 2003 World Athletics Championships, becoming the first Indian to win a medal at this event.
  5. Hima Das:
    6. Known as the "Dhing Express," she won the gold medal in the 400 meters at the 2018 World U20 Championships and multiple medals at the Asian Games.
