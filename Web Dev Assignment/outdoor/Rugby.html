Definition of Rugby

Rugby is a contact team sport that originated in England in the early 19th century. It involves two teams of 15 players (in rugby union) or 13 players (in rugby league) who compete to score points by carrying, passing, kicking, and grounding the ball in the opponent's try zone. The game emphasizes teamwork, strategy, and physical prowess.

Five Basic Rules in RugFivey

  1. Scoring:
    Points can be scored by grounding the ball in the opponent's try zone (a try), kicking the ball through the opponent's goalposts (a conversion, penalty kick, or drop goal).
  2. Passing:
    The ball must be passed backward or laterally. Forward passes are not allowed.
  3. Tackling:
    Players can tackle opponents to stop their progress, but tackles must be made below the shoulders and without dangerous play.
  4. Ruck and Maul:
    When a player is tackled and brought to ground, a ruck is formed, where players from both teams try to secure the ball. If the player remains standing, a maul is formed.
  5. Offside Rule:
    Players must be onside (behind the ball or behind the hindmost foot in a ruck or maul) to participate in play. Offside players cannot interfere with play until they return to an onside position.

Five Major Global Competitions in Rugby

  1. Rugby World Cup:
    The premier international rugby union competition held every four years, featuring teams from around the world.
  2. Six Nations Championship:
    An annual rugby union competition involving England, France, Ireland, Italy, Scotland, and Wales.
  3. The Rugby Championship:
    An annual rugby union competition featuring Australia, New Zealand, South Africa, and Argentina.
  4. World Rugby Sevens Series:
    An annual series of international rugby sevens tournaments featuring national teams.
  5. British and Irish Lions Tour:
    A quadrennial rugby union tour where a team composed of the best players from England, Ireland, Scotland, and Wales competes against southern hemisphere teams.

    The Indian men's rugby team won the silver medal, marking a significant achievement in the sport's growth in India.
  2. 2018 Asia Rugby Championship:
    The Indian women's rugby team recorded their first international win, defeating Singapore 21-19.
  3. Rugby India Development Programs:
