Definition of Billiards

Billiards is a game played on a rectangular table covered with a cloth, where players use cues to strike balls, aiming to score points by pocketing balls or achieving specific shots.

Five Basic Rules of Billiards

  1. Objective:
    Score more points than your opponent by pocketing balls or achieving specific shots.
  2. Cue Ball:
    Only the cue ball can be directly struck by the cue stick.
  3. Pocketing Balls:
    Points are scored by pocketing balls into the table's pockets.
  4. Turns:
    Players take turns to strike the cue ball.
  5. Fouls:
    Committing a foul results in a penalty, such as giving the opponent a ball-in-hand.

Five Prestigious Billiards Awards Globally

  1. World Billiards Championship:
    The most prestigious event in English billiards.
  2. U.S. Open 9-Ball Championship:
    A highly respected pool tournament.
  3. World Pool Masters:
    An annual event featuring top professional players.
  4. World Cup of Pool:
    A team event showcasing international talent.
  5. Mosconi Cup:
    A prestigious nine-ball tournament between Europe and the USA.
